## NTA 質疑応答事例 (`eval/v2/nta_gold.jsonl`)
> 出典：国税庁ホームページ「質疑応答事例」（https://www.nta.go.jp/law/shitsugi/）を加工して作成

Questions and answers are reproduced from the National Tax Agency website under its terms of use
(based on the Government Standard Terms of Use); each record was edited (split into query / answer
and linked to article ids).
